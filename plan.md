## Technical Specifications
- **Framework**: React with TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom tropical theme
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Images**: Pexels stock photography (beach/resort themed)
- **Currency**: Indian Rupees (₹)
- **Target Market**: Indian mid-level luxury pricing
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first approach with full responsiveness

## Design Principles
- **Apple-level aesthetics**: Meticulous attention to detail
- **Premium feel**: High-end visual presentation
- **Tropical theme**: Aqua, coral, sandy beige color palette
- **Micro-interactions**: Thoughtful hover states and animations
- **Visual hierarchy**: Clear typography and spacing systems
- **Performance**: Optimized loading and smooth interactions

## Content Strategy
- **Room Types**: Deluxe Ocean View, Premium Beachfront, Luxury Villa, Family Suite
- **Dining**: Beachside Cafe, Fine Dining, Poolside Bar, Room Service
- **Activities**: Water sports, beach volleyball, yoga, cultural tours
- **Amenities**: Infinity pool, spa, fitness center, kids club
- **Pricing**: Mid-level Indian hotel rates (₹8,000-25,000 per night)
